I am Syed Farhan Ahmad, PhD, a postdoctoral researcher in computational genomics with a focus on the non-coding repetitive genome. My work asks how transposable elements, satellite repeats, and other repetitive sequences influence gene regulation, chromatin state, genome evolution, and disease.

At St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, I work on cancer genomics questions related to repeat regulation, epigenetic control, and leukemia biology. I combine computational analysis with biological interpretation across RNA-seq, epigenomics, chromatin accessibility, genome assembly, and comparative genomics datasets.
