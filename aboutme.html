I am a computational genomics researcher studying the regulatory and evolutionary roles of repetitive DNA. At St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, my work focuses on transposable elements, cancer epigenomics, and leukemia biology.

My experience spans research design, sequencing analysis, experimental collaboration, manuscript development, and student mentoring. I work with Python, R, Bash, and HPC environments across transcriptomics, epigenomics, genome assembly, and comparative genomics.
