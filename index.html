Postdoctoral Research Associate · St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital

Decoding the regulatory logic of the repetitive genome.

I am a computational genomics researcher working at the intersection of repeat biology, epigenomics, genome evolution, and cancer biology. My research focuses on how transposable elements and other non-coding repetitive sequences shape genome function in development, evolution, and disease.

Research focus

Transposable elements in cancer

Studying how repeat-derived regulatory elements are epigenetically controlled and how their reactivation affects leukemia biology and therapeutic response.

Non-coding genome regulation

Integrating transcriptomic, epigenomic, and chromatin data to understand how repetitive DNA contributes to gene regulation and genome architecture.

Comparative genomics

Using genome assembly, repeat annotation, and evolutionary analysis to investigate how repetitive elements shape chromosomes across species.

Selected highlights
