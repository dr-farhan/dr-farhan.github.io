Syed Farhan Ahmad, PhD is a postdoctoral researcher in computational genomics at St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. His research focuses on transposable elements, repeat biology, cancer genomics, and the regulatory impact of the non-coding genome.

He received PhD training in Genetics at São Paulo State University (UNESP), where he worked on genome evolution, repetitive DNA, and B chromosome biology. His broader research combines genome assembly, comparative genomics, transcriptomics, epigenomics, and chromatin-focused analysis to understand how repetitive sequences shape genome function in evolution and disease.
